Compact battery optimiser — offline acceptance, 5 November 2026

Production core: components/charging_controller/compact_optimizer.h, shared
unchanged by native historical replay and firmware. 101 energy levels, float32,
2 recovery states, 7680-byte transient workspace; 2704-byte observation history.

Historical input: tests/backtest/se3-fridge-freezer-input.csv. SE3 NordPool-derived
quarter-hour prices obtained through Elklocka's Gridio source, not directly from
the exchange. SEK conversion reconstructed from daily reference averages, with
independent daily extrema and ECB FX plausibility checks in the original study.

Synthetic appliance load: randomized compressor cycles plus daily defrost,
original seed 20261105; minute power averaged into 15-minute intervals, scaled using
July only to 1.5 kWh/day. Evaluation actual 136.75349kWh. This is not measured freezer
consumption. Input SHA256: 14d912580ee5b53d6417207bec698d92c950eda7de8c5db0950fade3ef6955db.

Evaluation: 1 Aug–31 Oct 2026, 92 Stockholm calendar days, 2209 elapsed hours, 8836 slots;
next-day prices become available 13:15 local (assumption, not historical release
records). UTC timestamps preserve the autumn DST interval. July is prior data.
No future actual consumption is supplied to the controller. Off-only observed
appliance power is averaged across seven days, with 60 W boot fallback. Price
forecast is the median of seven days of completed hourly prices; continuation
value is the low quartile divided by charge efficiency. Initial forecast uses
already-published prices. Each 15 minutes the first action is recomputed at actual SOC.

Physical model: 4 kWh, 400 W AC charger exclusive of appliance load, 90% charge and
90% inverter efficiencies, 10 W DC inverter idle only on battery. Initial SOC 50%,
physical full 100%, 10% recovery start/20% release. Recovery is latched and modeled
with event-driven threshold crossings. Mains always supplies appliances AND
charges, capped only at full. No optional mains-without-charging mode, no BMS
MOS writes, no solar/export, price ceiling, deadline or daily SOC reset.
Prices: spot including 25% VAT + assumed 0.70SEK/kWh variable adder. Fixed fees,
demand charges, capital cost and battery wear excluded. Bill plus/minus final
inventory settlement at common July-derived 1.013528718SEK/battery-kWh; this is
neither export revenue nor physically restoring SOC. All policies start 50%.

Current firmware comparison: unchanged old production scheduling core replay,
same physical price/load assumptions, target 100%, ceiling 100 öre, deadline 07:00,
reserve10–20%. Saving 9.407SEK. Earlier target 90% variant saved 5.595SEK.
The target 100% reference corresponds to the user's fresh 5 November status.
The earlier high-resolution flat-forecast benchmark saved 46.896SEK, but used
longer/full-history estimates and a simpler reserve guard; it is a comparator,
not the exact compact implementation. Approximate hindsight reference 58.400SEK.

Resolution check savings: 101 nodes 44.666; 201 nodes 44.468; 401 nodes 44.629;
601 nodes 44.639; 1001 nodes 44.421. Float64 at 101 nodes gave the same savings and
trajectory as float32. This is empirical convergence, not an optimality proof.
At each fixed state, continuation is linearly interpolated rather than rounded
SOC. Independent short-horizon exhaustive checks found up to 0.0147 SEK first-action
regret at 101 nodes in tested partial quarters; 1001-node choices matched brute force.

These are diagnostics, not tuned settings recommendations. Hardware efficiency
and idle draw need real measurements before translating to household savings.

Verification: independent plant and numerical checks cover 502 plant transitions,
including narrow-band repeated cycles; 516 solver cases with exhaustive binary
short-horizon search, linear interpolation reference and partial first quarters;
future-price/consumption mutation checks; independent full-trace energy, supply,
reserve-latch and monetary accounting. Exact counts/current core hash are in
verification.json. Native ASan/UBSan controller tests include fault precedence,
manual/Automatic distinction, reserve hysteresis and observation history. UI
fixture tests cover desktop/mobile settings, modes and charts. No on-device
execution speed or runtime heap is inferred from desktop timing.

Limits: uniform demand within each 15 minutes; healthy exact BMS/meter readings;
charger fixed-rate until full rather than taper; no compressor surge/transfer
interruption model; ideal bypass has no extra standby draw. Actual firmware
samples BMS every 10 s and retains a two-minute ordinary switch cooldown. Recovery
is immediate at a fresh sample; slight threshold over/undershoot is possible.
Firmware fetch phase/latency, cached daily FX, cold start partway through a day
and observation quantisation differ from the standardized offline feed.
Study horizons are capped at evaluation end; the live controller has no artificial
end date. Results demonstrate performance in these scenarios, not future savings.
